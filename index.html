The Core Problem

Government measures speed of complaint closure (average 13 days, which is good). Nobody measures quality of closure. An officer can write "advised suitably," mark it resolved, and the citizen's problem remains unsolved. The citizen re-files, and the cycle repeats.

"Disposal-rate KPIs reward speed, not accuracy. Officers are scored on closing volume. Nothing in the current system scores whether the closure was true."
- APFC, EPFO (Primary Interview)
