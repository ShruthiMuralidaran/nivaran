Resolved · 70 and above

Hollow closure · below 40

Score = average of five criteria (addresses the complaint, specific action, grounded in policy, written for this case, clear next steps). Failure modes lower the ceiling: a reply that answers a different issue cannot score above 20; any serious failure mode caps it at 59; two or more cap it at 39. A failure mode only counts if the exact words that show it are quoted from the reply.
